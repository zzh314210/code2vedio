# Template assets

- `src/world_land.json`: Natural Earth 1:110m physical land, public domain. Derived from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson . Coastline only; no modern border is presented as a historical boundary.
- `frame-brief.png`, `frame-styles.png`, `frame-preview.png`: screenshots of this project’s public guest UI, captured 2026-10-19 with an original demonstration prompt; no account/private data.
